Model scope & field notes

Understand the limits.

Reference notes for the two categories supported by your ResNet-18 classifier.

Supported class · Disease

Black Rot

Look for yellow, V-shaped lesions starting along leaf margins, often accompanied by darkened veins. Severely affected leaves can wilt and turn brown.

Symptoms & reference photos · UMN Extension ↗

Supported class · Baseline

Healthy Leaf

Uniform green foliage free from marginal yellowing, darkened vascular veins, or necrotic lesions. Keep in mind that nutrient stress or insect feeding can mimic early symptoms.

Cauliflower care & disorders · UC IPM ↗
Binary classifier limitation: Because model5-singleTest.py uses a 2-class softmax head (Black Rot vs. Healthy), scores always sum to 100%. Conditions outside these two classes—such as downy mildew or insect damage—will still be forced into one of the two labels.

How this integrated pipeline works

Frontend → Flask API → PyTorch
01 /

Upload & resize

The browser posts your photo to /predict. PIL converts it to RGB and resizes it to 224 × 224 pixels.

02 /

ResNet-18 inference

The tensor is normalized with ImageNet mean/std and evaluated through your loaded Cauli-leaf_resnet18_model.pth weights.

03 /

Softmax screening

Logits are converted to class scores via softmax and returned as JSON to populate the insights panel.
